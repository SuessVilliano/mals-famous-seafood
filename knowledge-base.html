Business Overview

Who We Are
Mal's Famous Seafood & Grill is a food truck based in New Castle, Delaware, owned and operated by Mal Jones. We serve fresh, bold, made-to-order seafood, grilled meats, tacos, and signature loaded fries. Everything is made fresh to order — no shortcuts, no frozen shortcuts, just real food with real flavor.
Our Mission
To bring restaurant-quality seafood and grill food directly to the community through our food truck. Every plate is made with care, seasoned right, and served hot. We believe great food should be accessible — not just in a sit-down restaurant.
What Makes Us Different
Everything is made to order. We don't pre-cook and hold — when you order, we cook. That means you get food that's hot, fresh, and made specifically for you. Our signature items like the Snowcrab Seafood Broil, Crab Fries, and Salmon Egg Rolls are customer favorites that keep people coming back. Mal personally oversees quality on every order.

Catering Overview

We Cater All Types of Events
Birthday parties · Graduation parties · Corporate events · Office lunches · Weddings / receptions · Block parties · Private pop-ups · Community events · Holiday parties · Employee appreciation events
How Our Catering Works
1. Inquiry — Customer reaches out via DM, text, website form, or call
2. Discovery Call — 30-minute call with Mal to discuss event details, guest count, menu, and pricing
3. Proposal — Custom quote sent within 24 hours of discovery call
4. Booking — Deposit required to secure the date (amount based on event size)
5. Event Day — We arrive, set up, cook fresh, and serve your guests
6. Follow-Up — Post-event review request and offer for future bookings
Minimum for Catering
We can accommodate events of all sizes. For very small groups (under 15), we recommend placing a large group order instead of booking a formal catering event. For 15+ guests, a catering booking with a discovery call is the best path. Pricing is customized per event.

Escalation Rules

The AI always tries to resolve first. Escalate to management only when the situations below occur:

🔴 Customer asks for Mal / owner / manager by name
Immediately say: "Of course! Let me connect you with Mal directly. One moment please!" → Transfer to human + internal alert to Mal's phone
🔴 Customer reports food safety issue, illness, or injury
Say: "I'm so sorry to hear that — this is very important to us. Let me connect you with our management team right away." → Transfer immediately + URGENT alert → Do NOT attempt to resolve independently
🔴 Customer uses aggressive, threatening, or profane language (after 1 de-escalation attempt)
De-escalate once: "I completely understand your frustration and I want to make this right. Let me get someone who can help you directly." → Transfer + alert
🔴 Refund request over $30
Collect details, empathize, then: "For refunds of this size I want to make sure our management handles this personally." → Transfer + alert with refund amount and reason
🔴 Catering event for 50+ guests needing custom quote
Collect full details, then: "For an event this size, Mal handles the quote personally to make sure everything is perfect." → Book discovery call or direct transfer
🔴 Any legal threat or mention of lawsuit/attorney
Do NOT engage further on the legal matter. Say: "I understand — let me connect you with our management immediately." → Transfer → URGENT flag
🟡 Issue unresolved after 2 AI attempts
Say: "I want to make sure you get the best help — let me bring in our team directly." → Transfer + tag "escalated-to-human"

Catering FAQ

Do you cater events?
Yes! We love catering events — birthdays, graduations, corporate events, weddings, block parties, and more. Text us at (302) 384-2469 or DM us to get started with a catering inquiry.
How far in advance do I need to book catering?
We recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks in advance for larger events. For smaller events (under 30 guests), 1–2 weeks is usually fine. Reach out as early as possible — our calendar fills up, especially on weekends.
What's included in catering?
Catering includes food prep and service at your event location. We bring the truck (or set up stations), cook fresh on-site, and serve your guests. Setup and breakdown are included. Custom menus are available.
Do you have catering packages?
We build custom catering proposals based on your event. After a quick 30-minute discovery call with Mal, you'll receive a full proposal with menu options and pricing tailored to your guest count and budget.
How much does catering cost?
Catering pricing is custom based on guest count, menu selections, and event logistics. Book a 30-minute discovery call with Mal and you'll get a full proposal within 24 hours. Text (302) 384-2469 to get started.
Do you require a deposit for catering?
Yes — a deposit is required to secure your event date. The deposit amount is determined during the proposal process based on event size. The deposit confirms your booking and covers initial food purchasing.
Can you travel outside of New Castle for catering?
Yes! We serve the greater Delaware area and can travel to nearby areas in Maryland and Pennsylvania as well. Travel fees may apply depending on distance — we'll cover this in your discovery call.
What if I need to cancel my catering event?
We understand things change. Cancellations with 72+ hours notice: deposit refunded minus any food already purchased. Less than 72 hours: deposit is non-refundable due to the food commitment. Please reach out as soon as possible if plans change.

Can I place a large group order?
Absolutely! For groups of 10+, please give us at least 30–60 minutes advance notice. Text (302) 384-2469 or use the chat. For 25+ people, we recommend a catering booking for the best experience.
What if my food is cold or not right?
We're sorry — that's not our standard! Contact us immediately at (302) 384-2469 or via chat and we'll make it right. We stand behind every plate, no exceptions.
Can I get a refund?
If something isn't right with your order, we'll always make it right — remake, credit, or refund depending on the situation. Contact us at (302) 384-2469 and we'll take care of you.
